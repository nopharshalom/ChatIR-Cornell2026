# Decision rule recorded before development runs

Use the frozen development IDs and identical no-repeat screens for the matched comparisons. The primary score is the mean cumulative Hits@10 over rounds 1 through 5. The guardrail is cumulative Hits@10 at round 10. Advance a procedure when its paired primary score is strictly higher and its R10 count is at least the control count. Freeze eligible procedures and settings before any confirmation trajectories are inspected. Recommend a live 400 only if the same comparison improves on confirmation and passes the guardrail. Small numbers of affected targets remain provisional. For a pixel-specific display claim require improvement over both the disabled and caption-only arms. Test each mechanism independently.

Initial history arm excludes all previously displayed and rejected IDs, including the screen the user just rejected, before selecting ten MMR 0.85 representatives. Refill the shrinking evidence pool to its original size from the current ranking. The control selects evidence from its ordinary pool. Both display the top ten unseen images each round.

Initial pixel arm checks the most recent question if it asks a single color and its target answer identifies one basic color. Independently verify the asked attribute on unseen candidates ranked 8 to 13. Swap at most one explicit mismatch at ranks 8–10 for an explicit match at ranks 11–13. Preserve all other screen members. The caption-only arm uses the same question, answer vocabulary, candidates and decision rule on its own trajectory. The disabled arm uses the ordinary no-repeat display. Log raw candidate answers and abstain on uncertain or unsupported text.

If both history variants qualify, carry the one with higher development primary score, breaking ties by R10 and then fewer user questions. The contrast follow-up retains up to two nearest rejected captions from the ordinary pool and assigns the remaining slots to unseen MMR representatives. It addresses development case 343, where removing the original food captions changed the question to table material. If cutoff verification misses its advancement rule, test an alternative use of pixels in the previously frozen generic-fallback question selector. Compare indexed pixel answers with indexed caption answers and disabled visual selection under identical no-repeat displays. Use the same primary and guardrail criteria. Only the final frozen qualifying procedure from each direction may enter confirmation.
